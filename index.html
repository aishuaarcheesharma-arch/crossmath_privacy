Privacy Policy

App: Crossmath (com.aarchee.crossmath) · Developer: Aarchee Sharma
Effective date: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Crossmath app (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you play. By accepting this policy on first launch and using the App, you agree to it. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information we collect

Crossmath does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or location.

  • Game data stored on your device: your level progress, stars, hints, statistics, settings and your acceptance of this policy. This stays on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage deletes it.
  • Data collected automatically by advertising partners: the App shows ads using Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and process device and usage information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, app version, ad interactions, and diagnostic data, to serve ads, measure performance and prevent fraud.
  • Fonts: the App may load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is sent to Google servers when fonts are downloaded.

2. How information is used

  • To run the game and remember your progress and settings on your device.
  • To show banner, interstitial and rewarded ads that help keep the App free.
  • To measure ad performance, prevent fraud and keep the App secure.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not receive personal data collected by ad partners.

3. Advertising and your choices

  • Where required by law (for example the EEA, UK and some US states), the App shows a Google consent form before any ad is requested. You can choose whether ads are personalized.
  • You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices inside the App, when this option applies to your region.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are optional. You can use the App without watching them.
Google’s practices are described in Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Sharing of information

We do not share information with anyone ourselves. Information may be processed by Google (AdMob, Google Fonts, Google Play) under their own policies. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you delete the App or clear its data. Data held by Google is retained under Google’s policies. We hold no user database, so there is no account to delete.

6. Children’s privacy

Crossmath is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to withdraw consent and to object to processing. Because we do not hold your personal data, please use the Ad privacy choices and Android settings described above, or contact us and we will help you with any request.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the App, and game data is kept only on your device. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version and effective date will always be shown on this page. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
